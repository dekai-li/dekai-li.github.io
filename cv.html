Biography

Dekai Li is affiliated with the Department of Artificial Intelligence, School of Information and Communication Engineering, Hainan University. His research focuses on hyperspectral remote sensing, spectral–spatial representation learning, Mamba/state-space models, multimodal HSI–LiDAR fusion, contrastive learning, and limited-sample fine-grained classification, with applications to coastal and mangrove remote sensing.

Research Interests

Hyperspectral image classification · Mamba / state-space models · spectral–spatial fusion · HSI–LiDAR multimodal learning · Transformer and attention · contrastive learning · mangrove and coastal remote sensing
