Biography

Dekai Li is with the School of Information and Communication Engineering, Hainan University. His research focuses on hyperspectral remote sensing and intelligent Earth observation, including spectral–spatial representation learning, Mamba/state-space models, multimodal HSI–LiDAR fusion, contrastive learning, and fine-grained classification.

Research Interests

Hyperspectral image classification; Mamba/state-space models; spectral–spatial fusion; HSI–LiDAR multimodal learning; Transformer and attention; contrastive learning; mangrove and coastal remote sensing.
