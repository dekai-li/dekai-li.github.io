Biography

Dekai Li is with the School of Information and Communication Engineering, Hainan University. His research focuses on hyperspectral remote sensing and intelligent Earth observation, with particular interests in spectral–spatial representation learning, Mamba/state-space models, multimodal HSI–LiDAR fusion, contrastive learning, and fine-grained classification. His recent work also investigates coastal and mangrove remote sensing under complex environments and limited labeled samples.
