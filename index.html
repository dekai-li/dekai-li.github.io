Biography

Dekai Li is affiliated with the School of Information and Communication Engineering, Hainan University. His research focuses on hyperspectral remote sensing, spectral–spatial representation learning, Mamba/state-space models, multimodal HSI–LiDAR fusion, contrastive learning, and limited-sample fine-grained classification, with applications to coastal and mangrove remote sensing.
